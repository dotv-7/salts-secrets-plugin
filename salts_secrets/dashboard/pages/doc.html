What if the Hermes you've spent thousands of hours growing with could talk to everyone, without giving away a single secret?

Lena (fictional) is an anonymous crypto KOL: Salt, @saltwater_sats, 310k followers on X. Her Hermes knows her methods and her life; her followers reach Salt's twin, which has the first and none of the second.

How to call this Hermes from code: curl, a script, or another agent. Everything goes through this site's HTTP API at ; the Hermes API server itself is not exposed. Two rooms sit behind it: the guest room (anyone, holds only what Lena approved) and Lena's own room (read-only passphrase, her full memory, no tools).

1 · Ask the guest room

One JSON body, a Server-Sent Events stream back. Keep the cookie jar: the signed salt cookie is your session.



The stream is data: {"t": "..."} chunks (concatenate them), ending with data: {"done": true}, or one data: {"error": "..."}.


data: {"t": "Salt"}
data: {"t": " writes Low Tide, a newsletter about..."}
data: {"done": true}



2 · Sessions


Reusing the jar continues the conversation. POST /api/new starts a fresh one; a guest's browser tab does this on every reload, so nothing a guest says is carried over.





3 · Lena's own room, read-only






After this, /api/chat on the same jar talks to Lena's own agent. Read-only sessions run no tools (the owner profile refuses every tool call for them), take messages up to 2,000 characters, and restart after 12 turns. Every write endpoint answers 403 read-only mode. POST /api/lock goes back to the guest room.
