What if the Hermes you've spent thousands of hours growing with could talk to everyone, without giving away a single secret?

This plugin splits one Hermes by who is asking. Your own messages reach your profile, with all its memory, files and tools. Everyone else reaches a separate public profile that holds only what you approved on the Context tab, and has no tool that can open your files, sessions or memory. One gateway, one install; guests are not refused your secrets, they talk to an agent that never had them.

2 · Set up

--owner is your own identity on each platform; the plugin never guesses it. Use a handle for --owner-name, not your real name: it is how the public profile refers to you.



setup creates the public profile, installs this plugin there, writes the sender routes (your id to your profile, everyone else to the public one) and runs the check; if anything is missing, such as plugins.isolation: in_process, the failing line says how to fix it. It copies no model settings or keys; pick the model guests are answered with by running hermes -p public model. It is safe to run again.



3 · Context: decide what guests may know


    

  1. Every source your agent reads is listed in its own block: SOUL, MEMORY, USER, skills, workspace files, notes. Open one to see its entries; the left column is the raw original, the right column is what guests would get.
    2. 

  2. Opening the tab applies privacy rules only; nothing is sent to your model. Classify sorts the entries into A (publish as-is), B (needs a rewrite) and C (never public) with your own model.
    3. 

  3. Tick an entry to publish it. For B, Draft with Hermes writes the guest version with your profile's model; edit it freely, or tick AS-IS to send the original. Text marked "do not publish" never crosses.
    4. 

  4. Save & sync writes the public projection. Unticking something and syncing again removes it and deletes every guest session, so it does not live on in old transcripts.
    5. 



Every decision is logged in plugin-data/salts-secrets/approvals.jsonl. Add your own sensitive words (real name, family, places) to plugin-data/salts-secrets/privacy_rules.yaml.



4 · Permissions, Guests


Permissions sets the toolsets guests may use and turns guest access on or off. Guests lists every guest conversation, one row per session; open one to read it. Questions that probe for passwords, PINs, real names or addresses are flagged. Guest conversations are never injected into any prompt.



5 · From chat


Tell your own agent "let guests know I'm on holiday in May". It writes a guest-safe note and queues it; it cannot publish by itself. From your own DM, /salt lists the queue, /salt approve <id> publishes, /salt drop <id> discards.

7 · Limits


    

  • Native sender routes cover Telegram and Discord; other platforms go through a text-only second line.
    • 

  • In group chats you count as a guest: only your own DM reaches your profile.
    • 

  • One public profile serves all guests. They are isolated from you and from each other's history, but share one persona.
    • 

  • Isolation comes from profiles, configuration and plugins in one process, not a kernel boundary. Configuration can drift; that is what check is for.
    • 

  • Text already sent to your model provider or streamed to a chat cannot be recalled by a revoke.
    •
